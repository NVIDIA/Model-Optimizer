## Description: <br>
Guides an iterative, multi-candidate Model Optimizer quantization recipe search: choosing compute-vs-memory success metrics, selecting ModelOpt recipe baselines, designing AutoQuant/manual recipe deltas, interpreting sensitivity, and deciding the next candidate while delegating checkpoint generation, serving, evaluation, monitoring, and comparison to existing ModelOpt skills. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Owner
NVIDIA <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Developers and ML engineers use this skill to find the best quantization recipe for a model against a user-defined performance objective and accuracy threshold, planning baselines and candidates (e.g. NVFP4, FP8, INT4/AWQ, mixed formats) and deciding which candidate to try or promote next. <br>
